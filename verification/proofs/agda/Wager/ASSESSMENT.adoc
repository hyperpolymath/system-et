== Purpose

After the final verdict, the owner asked 4 questions: how far the wager got, what each repository can use, what work stays, and if the experiment was worth the time.
The answers were given in a chat on 2026-11-10. This file records them, so that a later session can read them.
The section "The result" says again what the verdict says. Each other section is a judgement of the session, not a checked statement.

== The result

W does not hold as written. W holds for the fragment with 2 additions.

. A newtype with a parameter needs trope states with an index, not 2 states.
. A whole program needs a grade algebra in which the grade `𝟙` is unrestricted. A coercion alone is typed in each grade algebra.

Agda has one program for each addition that shows why the addition is necessary. Other results:

* *Stage 6, part 2.* With an identity family, a local equality assumption is a variable at the grade `𝟘`, and the erasure of the proofs in casts is sound. The cost: equality evidence is a term. The verdict gives the facts for each answer to the question "does this add a relation?", and does not decide.
* *Stage 7.* A translated coercion computes `transport` along a path that is made with `ua`.
* *2 limits that the proofs showed.* The behaviour proof of Stage 6, part 2 does not use the grade. The erasure removes the evidence of a cast only, not evidence that is stored as data.

The development has 79 modules, no `postulate`, and a gate watch for each stage.

== Was the experiment worth the time?

On the idea: yes. The question had an answer that Agda can check, and the answer was not a plain yes. It corrected the theory in 3 places: the trope states, the role table, and the validity condition.

On the process: partly. A large part of the session went to CI drift. Each of the CI failures had a repair in the estate template; the repository had an old copy.

The main risk is breadth. `docs/status/PROOF-STATUS.adoc` marks ET-1, ET-2, ET-4 and ET-5 as mechanised, each with a limit, and the other obligations as open. anytype is at the scaffolding stage. `docs/PROOF-NEEDS.adoc` of affinescript calls its real lift "the bulk of the remaining work".
The wager helps the claim of the theory. It does not help to ship AffineScript v1. The session recommended that the owner choose one of those 2 goals for the next month.
